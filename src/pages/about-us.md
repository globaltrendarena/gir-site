Global Investment Reviews (GIR) covers the intersection of geopolitics and global capital: markets, banking, personal finance, and investment migration, written for readers who allocate capital across borders and need the signal without the noise.

We publish analysis, not advice. Every article is written to help readers understand a market or policy shift, not to recommend a specific transaction.
